# Project Context

## Audience

The primary reporter is a patient who noticed a possible problem while taking Castelzor. A caregiver reporting for another person is also supported. Reporters may be tired, worried, uncertain about dates, and completing the task on a phone.

## Job to be done

Help a patient or caregiver describe what happened, provide the details they know, and understand what would happen after finishing a demonstration.

## Product boundary

Castelzor is fictional. This client-only case study explores the shape and language of a post-market patient-reporting flow; it is not a safety intake system, medical service, FDA integration, or claim of regulatory compliance. The intended real-world process is described separately from what this prototype actually does.

## Information architecture

A welcome screen precedes six steps: reporter and patient, experience, chronology and outcomes, Castelzor details, other context, and contact plus review. A completion screen follows the local demo transition. Answers live only in reactive page memory.
